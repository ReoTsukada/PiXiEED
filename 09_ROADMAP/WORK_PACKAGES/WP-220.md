## Canonical objective

Implement an isolated, deterministic Direct Work workflow. It is not a general DM feature, not an
automatic Market Product, and not a production payment endpoint:

```text
Direct Work Request
  → Scope / Quote
  → Agreement
  → Milestones
  → Delivery references
  → Acceptance record
  → explicit Rights / License decision
  → Payment / append-only Ledger reconciliation
```

The same Core identity and permission vocabulary is used for requester, creator, request, quote,
agreement, milestone, delivery, acceptance, rights, payment, and ledger references. Raw private
request bodies, project content, credentials, Provider payloads, and payment secrets are excluded.

## Required implementation

- Versioned Request, Quote, Agreement, Milestone, Delivery, Acceptance, Rights, Payment, and
  append-only Ledger records with explicit lifecycle transitions and failure diagnostics.
- Direct Work authority separate from general DM, Market Product, Market Purchase, Subscription,
  and Notification Delivery.
- Integer minor-unit money with explicit currency and rounding policy. Client-provided amount,
  royalty, payout, license, owner, or entitlement scope is never authoritative.
- Quote total derived from canonical integer line items; checkout/payment command accepts only a
  server-resolved Quote/Agreement reference and opaque Provider correlation data.
- At-least-once payment-event handling: duplicate, out-of-order, forged, wrong request/account,
  unknown type, environment mismatch, and delayed events fail closed or become explicit no-ops.
- Append-only Ledger entries. Corrections use `REVERSAL` or `ADJUSTMENT` entries; old entries are
  never mutated. Payout is a separate future projection and its failure does not reverse the Work
  Request, Agreement, Delivery, or Acceptance.
- Explicit cancellation, dispute, rejected delivery, recovery/re-submission, and rights decision
  records. Payment success does not silently imply acceptance or a license grant.
- Default-off responsibility-specific flags and a Kill Switch. No Provider, Database, Storage,
  Notification, Market, or current-route call.
- Synthetic failure fixtures, targeted type/test/benchmark/bundle checks, and current-system
  non-intrusion evidence.

## Acceptance gates

1. Request → Quote → Agreement → Milestone → Delivery → Acceptance transitions are explicit,
   permission-scoped, deterministic, and separate from DM/Market.
2. Client cannot override server-resolved price, currency, royalty, payout, license, owner, or
   entitlement scope. Unsupported currencies and non-integer money fail closed.
3. Duplicate/out-of-order/forged/wrong-account/wrong-environment/unknown payment events do not
   double-create payment, ledger, acceptance, rights, or any Market entitlement.
4. Ledger is append-only and correction is represented by adjustment/reversal. Payout failure does
   not roll back Sale/Purchase/Entitlement or Direct Work acceptance.
5. Cancellation, dispute, rejected delivery, recovery, and explicit rights decisions preserve the
   prior valid record and do not delete history.
6. Direct Work lazy bundle is outside Draw2 Initial Editor, Game/Runtime, Market routes, and current
   production code. Approved WP-210 Market lazy baseline remains unchanged.
7. Approved Baseline Failure Identity remains 14/14 matched with new identity count 0. WP-000
   through WP-210 are not re-executed.
