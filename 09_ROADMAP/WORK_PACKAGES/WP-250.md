## Canonical objective

Implement an isolated, unloaded policy and projection Core for:

- server-resolved consent, region, age, provider, and viewport-aware public Ad eligibility;
- reserved, safe-area-aware lazy Ad slots without a provider SDK or request;
- shadow-only Revenue/Cost unit-economics projections and staged Ad revenue uncertainty;
- Organic versus explicitly labelled Sponsored Search presentation;
- versioned Moderation Case/Decision and non-replayable Admin Audit references.

This Work Package does not implement provider delivery, Analytics transport, Admin RPC/RLS,
moderation mutation, Ledger, Payout, checkout, or production integration.

## Implementation and invariants

`pixiedraw2/src/wp250-policy-economics-core.ts` is pure and runtime-independent. All five flags
default OFF, unknown flags and the kill switch fail closed, and rollback restores only a local
shadow snapshot. Public Ad surfaces are explicit; Draw2, Game, Audio, Direct Work, Checkout,
Account Security, private Project/Inbox, and Admin are always ad-free. Analytics consent remains
separate from Ad consent, with essential telemetry retained as a separate policy result.

Revenue streams are `ADS`, `MARKET_FEES`, `SUBSCRIPTIONS`, `DIRECT_WORK_FEES`, and
`BUILD_EXPORT_PAID_FEATURES`. Cost streams include Realtime, Database, Storage, Egress, Build,
Payment Provider, Moderation, and external paid services. Ad stages remain Gross Estimated,
Invalid-Traffic Adjusted, Provider Finalized, Withheld, and Paid. No projection can mutate Ledger
or Payout. Moderation stores bounded evidence references, never evidence body, and never performs
automatic canonical mutation.
