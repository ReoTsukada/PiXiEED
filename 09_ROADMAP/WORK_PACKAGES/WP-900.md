## Canonical role

Own the qualification matrix and acceptance-gap authority; never perform cutover. This definition is subordinate to `00_START_HERE/WORK_PACKAGE_REGISTRY.json`; the Registry owns status, dependencies, routes, write scope, and handoff truth.

## Gate authority

WP-900 is the entry and gap-authority gate for G0-G9. It must preserve predecessor status and provenance, classify each gap as BLOCKER/REQUIRED/OPTIONAL/UNTESTED, assign it to exactly one later package, and define the evidence needed to close it. It may issue `NOT_READY`, `BLOCKED`, or a decision request; it cannot grant Owner approval, change a current route, or authorize production action.

## Acceptance and evidence

- `WP900-MATRIX-001`: Reconcile `09_ROADMAP/TEST_MATRIX.md`, `docs/inventory/wp900-release-acceptance-matrix.json`, migration gates L0-L8, Registry, State, and Context. Record source path, gate, scope, priority, owner package, evidence class, and status for every item; no source status may be silently promoted.
- `WP900-GAP-001`: Rebuild or independently inspect the gap inventory. For each gap record finding identity, affected user journey, P0/P1/P2 class, dependency, required negative case, closure evidence, and rollback owner. Duplicate or contradictory entries are resolved by provenance, not deletion.
- `WP900-SOURCE-001`: Verify all required product, architecture, security, storage, native, performance, compatibility, migration, and release-operation sources exist. Missing or stale sources produce `BLOCKED`/`UNTESTED` with an Owner decision request, not PASS.
- `WP900-BOUNDARY-001`: Prove current PiXiEEDraw, existing routes, PXD, PiXiSYNC, Market, projects, assets, package, database, Storage, and production fallback are unchanged by qualification work. Record `git diff --check`, route inventory, current-system preservation result, and baseline failure identity; new baseline failures must be zero.
- `WP900-NONINTRUSION-001`: Attempt feature-flag OFF, kill-switch, unknown-flag, unauthorized-route, and accidental auto-start paths. Current path must remain usable and no next package may start automatically.

## Evidence contract

Each result is a source-traceable JSON/Markdown record with command/protocol, environment, fixture/hash, raw output, reviewer, timestamp, status, and linked finding IDs. Valid statuses are `PASS`, `FAIL`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED`, and `UNTESTED`; `UNTESTED` is not PASS. Synthetic, isolated, browser-only, or design-only evidence cannot satisfy a physical-device, production-equivalent, store, migration, or Owner requirement.
