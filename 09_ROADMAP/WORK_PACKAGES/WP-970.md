- `WP970-ROLLBACK-001`: Execute a documented rehearsal rollback to the previous artifact/config/data projection, then restore the forward path in isolation. Record trigger, owner, timestamps, hashes, data preservation, reconciliation, and observe period.

## Evidence contract

Every L0-L8 result records isolated environment ID, source/backup/artifact hashes, fixture manifest, command/protocol, before/after counts, exact reconciliation output, rollback and observation result, reviewer, timestamp, and status. A dry-run, plan, or synthetic result cannot satisfy an executed rehearsal. Production, real payment/payout, legal/accounting, and full fallback-window evidence stays `UNTESTED` when unavailable; baseline failure identity must match with new failures=0.
