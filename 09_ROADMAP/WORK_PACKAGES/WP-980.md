## Acceptance and evidence

- `WP980-BACKUP-001`: Confirm that WP-900 through WP-970 required blockers are closed or explicitly assigned; attach immutable evidence index, baseline identity, artifact/source hashes, rollback artifact, and backup/restore references. Any unresolved P0 blocks readiness.
- `WP980-IDENTITY-001`: Define server-controlled deterministic cohort, eligibility, exclusions, tenant/resource authorization, read/write flag separation, default-OFF state, and current-route fallback. Client-only cohort or flag is a failure.
- `WP980-MONEY-001`: Define measured thresholds for error, mismatch, security, latency, Long Task, memory, event backlog, reconciliation, and user-visible failure. Trigger/owner/action/observation window must be explicit; no threshold may be invented from an unmeasured result.
- `WP980-SHADOW-001`: Rehearse limited exposure in an isolated or explicitly authorized staging scope with kill switch, canary artifact, monitoring, alert, support trace, rollback, and post-rollback reconciliation. No real rollout occurs from this package.
- `WP980-RETIREMENT-001`: Define stop/rollback/continue criteria, named operator, communication path, retention, archive/restore, and fallback period. Record `READY_FOR_LIMITED_ROLLOUT`, `NOT_READY`, or `BLOCKED` as a recommendation only.
- `WP980-OBSERVE-001`: Specify observation duration, sampling, privacy-safe telemetry, success/error/recovery dashboards, and exact evidence required before WP-990. Absence of an observation run is `UNTESTED`.
