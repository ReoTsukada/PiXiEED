## Canonical scope

Implement a versioned Event Catalog and Envelope, trusted producer boundary, atomic domain-state plus
Outbox commit seam, durable Inbox consume reservation, idempotent delivery, aggregate ordering/gap handling,
causation/depth guards, bounded retry/DLQ, projection-only replay, and crash recovery. Tenant/resource
binding, authority context, event identity, and financial settlement boundaries remain server-owned. No
provider, Supabase, production database/Realtime, current route, or real user data.

## Required attack and recovery cases

- Concurrent same-event enqueue: one canonical event and one Outbox record.
- Duplicate delivery/provider-style input: no duplicate projection, entitlement, Ledger, or notification.
- Out-of-order/gap, unknown version, malformed envelope, wrong tenant/resource, forged actor, and invalid
  causation fail closed with typed diagnostics.
- Causation cycles and excessive depth reject without partial projection.
- Crash before commit, after domain commit, after Outbox append, and during Inbox consume recover without
  lost accepted events or double side effects.
- Concurrent single-use Inbox consume has one winner and releases reservation on failed lookup.
- Retryable backoff, non-retryable failure, bounded DLQ, cancellation, and replay with zero resend.
- Replay changes projections only; it cannot mutate financial authority or emit external delivery.
- PII, JWT, secrets, raw media, and provider credentials never enter payloads, logs, or diagnostics.
